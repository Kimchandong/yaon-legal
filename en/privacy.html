2. Information Shared with Businesses

  • Content that a Customer sends directly in consultations, reservations, or orders is viewed by the owner and staff of the relevant business.
  • When a Customer searches for nearby venues using AI, the request message and distance are shared with nearby businesses so that the Customer may receive invitations. The Customer's name and location coordinates are not shared with businesses. If MY › Settings › "Receive invitations from nearby businesses" is turned off, nothing is shared.
  • When a Customer makes a reservation, the no-show count (last 6 months) is provided to the venue that received the reservation. Only the combined count across all venues is shown; details such as at which venue or when a no-show occurred are not shown. If there are no no-shows, nothing is displayed.
  • Business statistics show only figures from which Customers cannot be identified (numbers of impressions and visits, distance ranges).

6. Security Measures
